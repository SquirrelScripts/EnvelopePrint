# Free Envelope Printer ✉️

> Print addresses on envelopes, postcards and label sheets without fighting Word. Paste a list, pick a size, drag to place, print.

**Use it → [squirrelscripts.github.io/EnvelopePrint](https://squirrelscripts.github.io/EnvelopePrint/)**

No install, no signup, and your addresses never leave your browser. (The hosted page loads its fonts from Google Fonts and counts anonymous visits with GoatCounter, with no cookies; neither sees your addresses.) Install it as an app from Chrome or Edge and it works offline.

## What it does

- **Print on anything:** 25 envelope sizes (business #6¼ to #14, A1 to A10 cards and invitations, catalog, DL/C4–C6) or a custom size; postcards (4×6 up to 5½×8½, with a divider line); and Avery label sheets (5160/8160, 5161, 5162, 5163, 5164, 5167 return address, and A4 L7160/L7163). Start part-way down a half-used label sheet.
- **Addresses in:** paste them with a blank line between each, paste cells from Excel or Google Sheets, or import a CSV. Google Contacts and Outlook exports are recognized, ZIPs that lost a leading zero get it back, and there's a ready-made spreadsheet template.
- **Clean lists:** Tidy up fixes capitals and spacing, applies postal abbreviations and state codes, and removes duplicates. Merge households turns people at the same address into one envelope ("John and Ann Smith" or "The Smith Family"). Addresses that look wrong (missing ZIP, unknown state, both a PO Box and a street) are flagged.
- **Saved lists and a card tracker:** keep separate lists (Christmas cards, wedding guests), tick who you sent to and who sent you one, skip anyone already sent when printing, and start next year's list from this year's.
- **Drag to place:** move the address, return address, logo and decoration on a live preview, or type exact measurements in inches or millimetres. Placement is remembered per size.
- **Looks the part:** everyday, elegant and script fonts; bold or bigger name line; left or centred; line spacing; ink colours; a logo; 35 flourishes (faith symbols such as a cross, dove, chalice and host, Sacred Heart and rosary, plus holidays and celebrations) in colour or matched to the ink; and a preview of kraft, blush or navy envelope stock.
- **Wedding style:** print the return address on the back flap as a second pass, placed on its own Back view.
- **Catches problems first:** warns if any address in the batch runs off the edge or is too long for its label, and shows the whole batch as thumbnails.
- **Test before you waste envelopes:** *Test on plain paper* prints the outline on a regular sheet to hold an envelope or label sheet against.
- **Printer adjustments:** nudge and rotate to match how your printer feeds envelopes. Set once, saved.
- **Print or save:** print the batch (Ctrl+P works too), or *Save PDF* to print later or elsewhere.
- **Backup:** download everything (lists, marks, logo, settings) to a file and restore it on another computer.

## Printing tips

In the print dialog, set **Margins** to **None** and **Scale** to **100%**, and turn off headers and footers. Chrome and Edge give the most reliable page sizing. If a browser won't size the page right, use *Save PDF* and print the PDF.

## Running it locally

Download `index.html` and open it in a browser; the app is that one file. (`sw.js`, `manifest.webmanifest` and the icons only matter for installing it from the website.)
